Embed This Map on Your Site

Partners are welcome to embed the live Workforce Navigator map on their own website using an <iframe>. The embedded map stays in sync automatically — any updates published here appear in the embed with no extra work.

Add the following HTML wherever you want the map to appear:

<iframe
    src="https://maconworkforce.com/"
    title="Macon Workforce Navigator"
    style="width: 100%; height: 800px; border: 0;"
    loading="lazy">
</iframe>

Notes on embedding:

  • Set a fixed pixel height (800px works well on most layouts) — the map does not currently resize to fit its container, so keep the iframe tall enough for the network graph and its category legend.
  • The embedded page includes the full site header, navigation, and footer since there's no stripped-down embed view yet. If you need a chrome-less version for a tighter embed, let us know and we can look into adding one.
  • The map requires JavaScript and loads its data from this site, so the embedding page must allow cross-origin iframes (this is the default for most site builders and CMSs).

Project Overview

The Macon Workforce Navigator is an interactive visualization showcasing the interconnected network of workforce development resources in the Greater Macon region. This comprehensive tool helps job seekers, employers, and community partners understand and navigate the rich ecosystem of training, education, and support services available locally.

From higher education institutions to specialized job training programs, faith-based initiatives to re-entry services, this map demonstrates how 31 workforce assets work together to build and sustain Macon's economic future.

This map is only as accurate and complete as the community keeps it. New workforce organizations start up, programs change, and contact details go stale — we rely on job seekers, employers, and partner organizations to flag what's missing or out of date so the Navigator stays a resource people can trust.

Greater Macon Chamber of Commerce

The Greater Macon Chamber of Commerce serves as the driving force behind regional workforce development initiatives. As the convening organization for employers, educational institutions, and community partners, the Chamber works to align workforce programs with economic growth strategies.

Key workforce initiatives include:

  • Development of the Macon Workforce Navigator
  • Creation of the Greater Career Works platform
  • Choose Macon talent attraction campaign
  • Leadership Macon professional development
  • Employer-driven workforce pipeline development
